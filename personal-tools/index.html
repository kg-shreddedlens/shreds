Ken Greenwood personal tools

This page describes "Ken Cursor Gmail", a private Google sign-in used only by Ken Greenwood on his own computer. It is not a public product and does not accept other users.

It lets Ken's own scripts read and send his own Gmail and read the free/busy times of his own Google calendars, so that his availability can be shown on his work calendar.

See the privacy policy. Contact: ken.greenwood@gmail.com.
