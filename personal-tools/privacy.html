Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

"Ken Cursor Gmail" is a personal tool used only by Ken Greenwood with his own Google account. No one else's data is collected.

What it accesses

  • Ken's own Gmail, to read, draft and send his own messages and attachments.
  • The free/busy times of Ken's own Google calendars. Event titles, attendees, locations and notes are not requested.

Where the data goes

Data is processed on Ken's own computer. Busy time ranges are copied, as untitled "Busy" blocks, to Ken's own work calendar. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Sign-in tokens are stored only on Ken's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
